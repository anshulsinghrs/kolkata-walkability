Methodology

PWS integrates four built-environment indicators extracted from GSV imagery — sidewalk, greenery, streetlight, crowdedness — weighted by coefficients from a binary-logit stated-preference model.

Higher scores indicate environments perceived as more walkable. Inspired by CTstreets (Connecticut Walkability Map).
